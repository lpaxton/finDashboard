Reading this with the dev team. The backbone is the script: steps 1.1 → 3.6 in order. A story here is a Jira story in all but the template: the ask becomes as Dana, I can…, "done looks like" becomes the acceptance criteria, the change tag is the first sizing, and the click-map row points at what already exists so nobody re-specifies it. The three beats are the epics.

Where AI is on this path: one place still to build (S-07, next steps from the meeting record). Everything else the demo shows as the platform's voice already runs, live or offline. Ask is not on the path; keep it off until the connectors exist.
