What the map says

24 of 44 steps use AI through one of seven capabilities. The other 20 are built without AI: forms, settings, the lead card, scheduling and signing. They are still components to design and build. The three intake forms are in the Capture row.

What the POC build leaves out

Professional development and business operations are not being built in the POC. SIMGPT is in the baseline as an option inside prospect meeting prep, connected through its API. That is part of this journey.

After the signature

The platform does not end there. The portfolio platform does the money work whenever it is needed; a layer of the platform stays over the whole relationship, notices what needs attention and prepares it for the advisor.
